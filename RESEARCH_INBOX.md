## Search plan

At the first review, cover the gap from the relevant topic's last verification date through the search date. Do not use the monthly window to skip the July-to-October backlog.

For ongoing reviews, use a 45-day publication/update window and document indexing lag. Adapt these starting queries rather than treating them as exhaustive:

- Broad geroscience: `(aging OR ageing OR geroscience OR longevity) AND (randomized OR trial OR frailty OR multimorbidity OR lifespan OR mortality)`
- Translation: `(rapamycin OR sirolimus OR senolytic OR "IL-11" OR reprogramming) AND (trial OR safety OR replication OR lifespan)`
- Metabolic interventions: `(tirzepatide OR semaglutide OR "GLP-1") AND (mortality OR frailty OR cognition OR "physical function" OR aging)`
- Measurement: `("epigenetic clock" OR "biological age" OR DunedinPACE) AND (validation OR randomized OR surrogate)`

Also search the exact trial name, registry ID, molecule synonyms, and key authors. Check corrections/retractions and reference lists. Do not filter out animal or non-randomized studies when the question is mechanistic; label their evidentiary role.

Record registry updates separately from publication searches. A planned completion date is not a promised results-publication date.

## Decision rules

| Decision | Use when |
|---|---|
| UPDATE | The source materially changes a conclusion, its uncertainty, safety, or trial/regulatory status |
| WATCH | A relevant preliminary discovery, preprint, protocol, or incomplete result needs follow-up |
| NO CHANGE | Relevant evidence was reviewed but does not materially change the synthesis; record why |
| OUT OF SCOPE | No clear connection to aging biology, healthspan, or interpretation; link to a supporting guide only if useful |
| UNRESOLVED | The primary source is inaccessible, identifiers conflict, or the claim cannot yet be checked |

New research should earn a place by what it changes. A negative result or retraction may be more consequential than a positive small study.
